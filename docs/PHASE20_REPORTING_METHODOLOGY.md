## 1. System Philosophy & Objectives

Tactical reporting in elite association football often suffers from two fundamental deficiencies:
1. **Uncalibrated subjectivity:** Tactical narratives and recommendations lack deterministic traceability back to quantitative spatiotemporal telemetry.
2. **Epistemic confusion:** Pure kinematic observations are frequently conflated with simulated model outputs or speculative coaching hypotheses.

Phase 20 resolves these deficiencies by establishing a mathematically rigorous, deterministic, professional tactical reporting pipeline. Every statement, visual diagram, and strategic recommendation is classified by evidence certainty, calibrated to official Metrica tracking telemetry, and formatted using an editorial, publication-grade design system suited for head coaches, tactical analysts, and sporting directors.

## 2. Epistemic Evidence Classification Hierarchy

Every analytical claim and metric presented in a TacticalGhost report is tagged with one of four epistemic tiers:

| Tier | Category Identifier | Description | Mathematical / Empirical Basis | Example Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | `OBSERVED_TRACKING_TELEMETRY` | Direct optical coordinate tracking measurements. | Measured positions $\vec{p}(t)$, velocities $\vec{v}(t)$, acceleration $\vec{a}(t)$, aggregate sprint distance. | Sprint count $\ge 25.2\,\text{km/h}$, trajectory lengths, pass reception coordinates. |
| **Tier 2** | `DETERMINISTIC_MODEL_ESTIMATE` | Deterministic mathematical formulations applied to tracking telemetry. | Spearman (2018) Pitch Control, Voronoi/Delaunay tessellations, convex hull spatial stretch index. | Pitch control probability $P_{\text{Home}}(\vec{x})$, defensive line height variance. |
| **Tier 3** | `TACTICAL_INTERPRETATION` | Algorithmic heuristic classifications derived from multi-frame tracking events. | Decoy run classification, pressing trap triggered, spatial overload identification. | Decoy run score $\ge 0.70$, resting defensive 3+2 structure classification. |
| **Tier 4** | `HYPOTHETICAL_COACHING_ASSUMPTION` | Expert coaching interventions, countermeasure directives, or simulated tactical adjustments. | Perturbed parameter simulations, coach drill specifications, tactical role assignments. | "Deploy inverted fullback into central corridor to negate half-space overload". |

## 3. Seven Professional Report Types

### 3.1 Type A: Match Intelligence Report (16 Structured Sections)
Designed for comprehensive post-match analysis across the full 90-minute encounter:
1. Executive Summary & Match Overview
2. Match Metadata & Telemetry Provenance
3. Pitch Control Dominance & Territorial Territory Breakdown
4. Space Creation & Decoy Run Analysis
5. Defensive Compactness, Stretch Index & Line Height
6. Defensive Vulnerabilities & Broken Line Episodes
7. High-Intensity Pressing & Cover Shadow Exploitation
8. Passing Network, Lane Viability & Central Corridor Penetration
9. Numerical Overloads & Flank Isolation
10. Rest-Defense Structure & Defensive Transition Security
11. Set-Piece & Dead-Ball Spatial Structure
12. Physical Output & Kinematics Overview
13. Expected Threat (xT) & Spatial Opportunity Conversion
14. Key Tactical Moments & Sequence Timelines
15. Strategic Coaching Recommendations & In-Game Countermeasures
16. Telemetry Limitations & Epistemic Disclosures

### 3.2 Type B: Tactical Analysis Report
Focused on recurrent tactical patterns identified in Phase 16:
- Recurrent sequence verification (multi-sequence recurrence analysis).
- In-possession buildup mechanics, overload generation, and progression channels.
- Transition dynamics, counter-press effectiveness, and ball recovery latency.
- Zone 14 exploitation frequency and defensive perimeter destabilization.

### 3.3 Type C: Opponent Intelligence Report
Dedicated opponent tactical dossier based on Phase 17 Opponent Tactical DNA:
- Tactical DNA signature across 4 analytical dimensions (Build-up, Pressing, Rest-Defense, Spatial Vulnerability).
- Structural weaknesses, defenseless corridors, and line-breaking access channels.
- Concrete defensive countermeasures with anticipated tactical trade-offs.

### 3.4 Type D: Coach Strategy Gameplan
Actionable pre-match dossier designed for the manager and coaching staff:
- Evaluation of 7 standard tactical formations (4-3-3, 4-2-3-1, 4-4-2, 3-4-3, 3-5-2, 4-1-4-1, 5-3-2) scored orthogonally across Role Suitability, Rest-Defense, Flank Overload, and Opponent Exploitation.
- Recommended Starting XI with explicit role assignments, pitch coordinates, and tactical instructions.
- Targeted exploitation plans across 4 spatial corridors (Left Half-Space, Central Pocket, Right Flank Overload, Deep Channel Behind Backline).
- Transition protocols featuring a calibrated 3+2 Rest-Defense structure.
- 4 tactical training drills with field dimensions, player constraints, and coaching objectives.

### 3.5 Type E: Player Scouting Dossier
Deterministic scouting profile derived from Phase 18 anonymous tracking intelligence:
- Player identity verification with strict anonymous tracking labels (`Home_11`, `Away_24`).
- Classification across 9 tactical role archetypes with fit probabilities:
  1. Box-to-Box Engine
  2. Deep-Lying Playmaker
  3. High-Press Disruptor
  4. Decoy Space Creator
  5. Half-Space Infiltrator
  6. Wide Overloader
  7. Rest-Defense Anchor
  8. Defensive Line Commander
  9. Inverted Wing Threat
- 6-axis performance radar chart: Attacking Space Creation, Defensive Recovery, Pressing Intensity, Tactical Discipline, Ball Retention Under Pressure, and Kinematic Output.
- Transfer suitability, tactical adaptability, and epistemic limitation warnings.

### 3.6 Type F: Multi-Match Comparison Report
Comparative analysis bridging multiple matches (e.g. Game 1 vs. Game 2):
- Cross-match dataset compatibility score audit with failing-dimension penalties.
- Opponent Tactical DNA evolution and structural drift across matches.
- Persistent spatial seams and recurrent structural vulnerabilities.

### 3.7 Type G: Executive Summary Report
High-density 2-page decision brief for sporting directors, owners, and senior executives:
- Top 3 critical analytical findings.
- Top 3 structural vulnerabilities requiring immediate intervention.
- Immediate strategic action plan with prioritization and risk ratings.

## 4. Pure Vector SVG Visualization Specifications

To guarantee crisp rendering across 4K displays, A4 printing, and responsive viewports without pixelation or external image dependencies, all tactical diagrams are rendered as pure vector SVGs:

### 4.1 Pitch Base Coordinate System
All pitch visualizations conform to the official FIFA pitch dimensions:
- Coordinate space: $x \in [0.0, 105.0]\,\text{meters}$, $y \in [0.0, 68.0]\,\text{meters}$.
- Orientation: Attacking left-to-right (Home attacking towards $+x$).
- Standard markings: Penalty boxes ($16.5\,\text{m} \times 40.32\,\text{m}$), Goal boxes ($5.5\,\text{m} \times 18.32\,\text{m}$), Center circle ($r = 9.15\,\text{m}$), Penalty spots ($11.0\,\text{m}$ from goal lines).

### 4.2 Formation Slot Renderer (`render_formation_svg`)
- Player nodes rendered at exact mathematical centroid coordinates $(x_i, y_i)$.
- Color-coded circles: Primary home squad (`#ea580c`), opponent defensive block (`#3b82f6`).
- Role archetype annotations and anonymous player tags rendered with high-contrast SVG text labels.

### 4.3 Corridor Exploitation Diagram (`render_corridor_exploitation_svg`)
- Highlights the 4 critical attacking corridors with semi-transparent overlay polygons:
  - Corridor 1: Left Half-Space ($x \in [65.0, 88.5]$, $y \in [45.0, 58.0]$)
  - Corridor 2: Zone 14 Central Pocket ($x \in [70.0, 88.5]$, $y \in [20.4, 47.6]$)
  - Corridor 3: Right Flank ($x \in [60.0, 95.0]$, $y \in [5.0, 18.0]$)
  - Corridor 4: Deep Channel Behind Backline ($x \in [88.5, 105.0]$, $y \in [15.0, 53.0]$)
- Overlay directional penetration arrows with dashed progression pathways.

### 4.4 Radar Chart (`render_radar_chart_svg`)
- Equiangular 6-axis polygon with concentric grid rings at 20%, 40%, 60%, 80%, and 100%.
- Semi-transparent fill (`rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.25)`) with crisp perimeter line (`#ea580c`, 2.5px).
- Numerical value labels at each vertex.

### 4.5 Spatial Zone Heatmap (`render_zone_heatmap_svg`)
- $6 \times 4$ spatial grid partition of the pitch (24 tactical zones).
- Gradient fill mapping pitch control probability: `#3b82f6` (Away dominance) $\rightarrow$ `#f8fafc` (Contested) $\rightarrow$ `#ea580c` (Home dominance).

## 5. Dual-Engine PDF Generation Architecture

To ensure high-fidelity A4 document exports across diverse runtime environments, TacticalGhost employs an automated fallback dual-engine architecture:

```
                          [Report Data Model]
                                   |
                                   v
                         [HTML Report Exporter]
                     (A4 @media print CSS Styles)
                                   |
                      +------------+------------+
                      |                         |
                      v                         v
              [Engine 1: Primary]       [Engine 2: Fallback]
             Chrome Headless CLI         ReportLab Vector Engine
           `--print-to-pdf-no-header`    (In-process pure Python)
                      |                         |
                      +------------+------------+
                                   |
                                   v
                           [Output A4 PDF]
```

1. **Engine 1 (Chrome Headless):**
   - Invokes the local Chrome binary with `--headless=new --print-to-pdf --no-pdf-header-footer --print-background`.
   - Produces pixel-perfect reproductions of HTML/CSS layouts, embedded SVGs, and typography.
2. **Engine 2 (ReportLab Fallback):**
   - Automatically executes if Chrome is unavailable or fails in sandboxed environments.
   - Built on `reportlab.platypus` with `SimpleDocTemplate`, `Paragraph`, `Table`, `KeepTogether`, and `HRFlowable`.
   - Includes custom canvas headers, confidentiality notices, and dynamic page numbering ("Page X of Y").

## 6. Strict Data Integrity, Zero Fabrication & Audit Rules

Every generated tactical report is validated against non-negotiable scientific integrity rules prior to storage and export:
1. **Zero Fabrication Guarantee:** No player names, ages, market values, or match scores may be fabricated. All player references must use verified tracking identifiers (`Home_11`, `Away_24`).
2. **Coordinate Bounding Verification:** All spatial coordinates in visualizations and claims must satisfy $0.0 \le x \le 105.0\,\text{m}$ and $0.0 \le y \le 68.0\,\text{m}$.
3. **Metric Finiteness:** No metric or calculation may evaluate to `NaN`, `+Inf`, `-Inf`, or negative variance.
4. **Section Completeness:** Reports must contain their full canonical section counts (e.g. exactly 16 sections for Match Intelligence).
5. **Epistemic Traceability:** All recommendations and claims must declare their evidence category and source intelligence engine.
